January arrives and suddenly everyone thinks salvation is hiding inside a HIIT circuit. New year, new sweat, new pressure to “push harder.” But when your nervous system is already juggling stress, fatigue, and winter overwhelm, intense workouts can actually promote inflammation rather than help you fight it. Here’s the part nobody tells you: Slow, controlled movement can reduce inflammation more effectively than all-out intensity, especially when your system is running on empty.
