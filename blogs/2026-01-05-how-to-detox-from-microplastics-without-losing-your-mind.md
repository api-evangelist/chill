For years, microplastics lived in the background of the wellness conversation as a kind of vague environmental guilt, grouped with plastic waste, plastic bottles and the food chain. But then the research sharpened: plastic particles aren’t just in the environment; they’re in the human body - in our digestive tract, gut microbiome, bloodstream and, in some studies, even breast milk. Cue the headlines.
