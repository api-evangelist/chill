January hits and suddenly everyone is convinced their stomach is a crime scene. Searches for how to reduce bloating surge, influencers push “debloating workouts,” and the world collectively panics about abdominal bloating as if it’s a moral failure rather than a normal response of the digestive system. Here’s the truth: Feeling bloated after December doesn’t mean your digestive tract is broken — it means you’re human.
